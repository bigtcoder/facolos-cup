RULES & FAQ

Beginner is DUPR 2.0–2.5, Intermediate 2.5–3.0 and Advanced 3.0–3.5. Use your DUPR rating; if you don't have one, self-rate honestly. Players on a boundary rating (2.5 or 3.0) can choose either division. The team's higher-rated player sets the division. The organiser may move players or teams between divisions to keep play fair.
